# Research Intelligence Toolkit

An AI-powered research methodology and statistical analysis toolkit.

This repository turns applied research experience into reusable, evidence-led skills. It focuses on defensible research decisions rather than mechanical threshold checking.

## Skills

### Statistical Diagnostic Analyst
Location: [`statistical-diagnostic-analyst/`](statistical-diagnostic-analyst/)

Diagnoses survey measurement quality, regression assumptions, influential observations, mediation/moderation models, cross-group comparability, and robustness.

### Research Design Architect
Location: [`research-design-architect/`](research-design-architect/)

Structures research problems, gaps, questions, theory, constructs, sampling, feasibility, ethics, and supervisor feedback into an aligned research design.

### Systematic Review Analyst
Location: [`systematic-review-analyst/`](systematic-review-analyst/)

Supports database search strategies, transparent screening, PRISMA 2020 reporting, evidence synthesis, and defensible research-gap identification.

**Core principles**
- Never invent search counts, dates, screening decisions, or study characteristics.
- Distinguish database results, exported records, screened records, and prioritised reading lists.
- Report reviewer roles and screening stages accurately.
- Build research-gap claims from patterns in the evidence, not assumptions about what has not been studied.

## Case-based documentation

The statistical skill includes anonymised examples from a virtual-idol tourism research project:
- Pilot study (N=50): item diagnostics, regression, multicollinearity, influential observations, and sensitivity analysis.
- Main study (N=300; sensitivity N=250): CFA, CR, AVE, HTMT, measurement invariance, mediation, moderation, and pilot-wave sensitivity.

The design and systematic-review skills also include case-based guidance. Project figures are examples from the documented work, not universal thresholds or default recommendations.
