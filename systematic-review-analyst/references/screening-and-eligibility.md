# Screening and Eligibility

## 1. Define criteria before screening

Criteria should be operational, mutually understandable, and aligned with the review question. Common dimensions include:

- Population or audience.
- Phenomenon or exposure.
- Context or setting.
- Outcomes or constructs.
- Study design and publication type.
- Language and publication period.
- Minimum information required to assess relevance.

Avoid criteria that merely restate a desired conclusion.

## 2. Example eligibility matrix

| Dimension | Include when | Exclude when |
|---|---|---|
| Topic relevance | Directly addresses the review phenomenon | Only mentions a keyword incidentally |
| Context | Fits the specified domain or setting | Outside the defined context |
| Evidence type | Meets the chosen study/publication criteria | Editorial, commentary, or other excluded type |
| Language/date | Meets pre-specified limits | Outside stated limits |

Replace these generic examples with criteria specific to the review.

## 3. Screening stages

A typical workflow may include:
1. Import records.
2. Deduplicate.
3. Title screening.
4. Abstract screening.
5. Full-text eligibility assessment, if conducted.
6. Final inclusion.

Not every review uses every stage in the same way. Report only stages that actually occurred.

## 4. Reviewer process

Document:
- Number of reviewers at each stage.
- Whether screening was independent or sequential.
- Whether a pilot calibration exercise was conducted.
- How disagreements and uncertain records were resolved.
- Whether an additional reviewer adjudicated disagreements.

If one person screened all records, say so. Do not imply inter-rater reliability or independent duplicate screening.

## 5. Exclusion reasons

At full-text stage, use a manageable set of mutually interpretable reasons, such as:
- Wrong population/context.
- Wrong phenomenon.
- Wrong outcome.
- Ineligible publication type.
- Insufficient relevant evidence.

Record one primary reason per excluded report if that is the chosen protocol. The categories and counts must reflect actual decisions.

## 6. Unavailable full texts

If a full text cannot be obtained, record the report as not retrieved where appropriate. Do not silently treat it as assessed and excluded for an eligibility reason.

## 7. Decision log

| Record ID | Stage | Decision | Reason | Reviewer | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unique identifier | Abstract | Include / Exclude / Unclear | Specific criterion | Initials | Date |

Retain an "unclear" route for records that require further inspection. Do not force uncertain records into a convenient category.

## 8. Protocol deviations

If criteria change after screening begins:
- Describe what changed.
- State when and why it changed.
- Identify whether earlier records were re-screened.
- Explain the potential effect on selection.

Do not silently rewrite the protocol to match the final paper set.
