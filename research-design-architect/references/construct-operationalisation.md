# Construct operationalisation

For each construct, document:
1. Conceptual definition.
2. Dimensions and boundaries.
3. Role in the model.
4. Source scale or item-development rationale.
5. Adaptations and language versions.
6. Item wording and response scale.
7. Scoring and reverse coding.
8. Expected overlap with neighbouring constructs.

## Wording consistency
Check target, time frame, tense, specificity, and strength of commitment. Items intended to form one scale should not quietly measure different stages of a behavioural process.

## Behavioural distinctions
Do not conflate:
- exposure with engagement;
- engagement with immersion;
- destination image with intention;
- intention with preparatory planning;
- preparatory planning with actual travel.

A study of saving travel posts, searching flights, or drafting itineraries measures planning-related actions, not necessarily completed travel.
