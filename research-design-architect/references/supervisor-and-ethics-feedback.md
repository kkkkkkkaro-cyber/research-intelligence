# Supervisor and ethics feedback integration

Treat feedback as a constraint to understand and evaluate. Do not infer approval, disapproval, or hidden intentions beyond what was actually said.

## Response workflow
1. Quote or accurately paraphrase the concern.
2. Identify whether it concerns theory, measurement, sampling, analysis, feasibility, risk, or compliance.
3. Separate mandatory ethics requirements from methodological recommendations and personal preferences.
4. Evaluate the rationale and downstream implications.
5. Offer a response with evidence and a clear decision.
6. Update the research decision log and affected documents.

## Professional communication
Emails should be concise, respectful, specific, and action-oriented. State the decision needed, the relevant evidence, and the proposed next step. Avoid defensive language and avoid presenting unresolved options as settled facts.

## Ethics
Never claim ethics approval before written approval. Ensure recruitment materials, consent information, data handling, retention, third-party platforms, and participant eligibility match the approved protocol.
