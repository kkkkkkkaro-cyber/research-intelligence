# Research Design Architect

A reusable skill for turning an early research idea into a coherent, feasible, ethically defensible design.

It supports research-problem framing, gap development, theory selection, conceptual models, construct operationalisation, survey design, sampling, feasibility, ethics, and supervisor-feedback integration.

**Key principle:** align the research question, theory, constructs, measures, sample, analysis, and claims before collecting data.
