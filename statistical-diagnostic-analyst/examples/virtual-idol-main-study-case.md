# Case: Virtual-idol tourism-intention main study (N=300; sensitivity N=250)

## Context
Japan n=150 and China n=150, including 50 Chinese pilot responses. The pilot led to wording revisions for FP2 and TI2. The report also repeated analyses excluding the pilot wave (China n=100; total N=250).

## Reported methodological sequence
1. Revise the research framework after destination-image discriminant-validity concerns.
2. Assess measurement models using CFA, CR, AVE, and HTMT.
3. Assess measurement invariance for Japan–China comparisons.
4. Test mediation of FP → PI → TI.
5. Test country moderation and moderated mediation.
6. Treat additional moderators as exploratory.
7. Test sensitivity to influential cases and to excluding the pilot wave.

## Interpretation safeguards
- Do not claim that non-significant moderated mediation proves identical indirect effects.
- Do not compare groups structurally without explaining measurement comparability.
- Distinguish confirmatory hypotheses from exploratory moderators.
- Disclose that the pilot wave used earlier wording and show the N=250 sensitivity results.
- Cross-sectional self-report data support associations, not definitive causal claims.

This example documents the workflow reported in the analysis file; it is not an independent reanalysis of the raw data.
