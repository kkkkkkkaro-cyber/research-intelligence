# Reporting and decision log

For every consequential decision, record:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Issue | What diagnostic raised the question? |
| Evidence | Exact statistic, output, sample, and model |
| Alternative explanations | Coding, wording, translation, theory, sampling, model |
| Action | Retain, revise, investigate, exclude, or re-specify |
| Rationale | Why this action is defensible |
| Sensitivity check | What alternative was tested? |
| Reporting | How the decision and uncertainty will be disclosed |

Use cautious language:
- “was associated with” rather than “caused” for cross-sectional data;
- “sensitive to influential observations” rather than “invalid”;
- “evidence did not establish a difference” rather than “the groups are identical”;
- “exploratory finding” when not pre-specified.

Never invent missing statistics. If individual diagnostics, thresholds, or model details are unavailable, state that they were not present in the supplied output.
