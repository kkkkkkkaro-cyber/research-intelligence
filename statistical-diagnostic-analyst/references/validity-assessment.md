# Construct validity assessment

## Keep the concepts distinct
- Internal consistency: whether items cohere.
- Convergent validity: whether indicators represent their intended construct.
- Discriminant validity: whether constructs are empirically distinguishable.
- Multicollinearity: whether predictors overlap enough to destabilise regression estimates.
- Global model fit: whether the specified model reproduces the observed data adequately.

## Recommended checks
For latent-variable models, inspect indicator loadings, CR/omega as appropriate, AVE, and HTMT with confidence intervals. Consider theoretical content and alternative model comparisons. For group comparisons, test measurement invariance before comparing latent relationships or means.

## Decision logic
- Do not treat a single cutoff as an automatic pass/fail rule.
- If two constructs are not empirically distinguishable, investigate item overlap, construct definitions, model specification, and theory.
- Revise or remove a construct only with a documented theoretical and empirical rationale.
- VIF can diagnose predictor collinearity in a regression equation; it does not establish discriminant validity.

## Case illustration
The later virtual-idol analysis removed Destination Image from the revised framework after discriminant-validity concerns. The skill should treat this as a model-revision decision supported by measurement evidence, not as a generic instruction to drop constructs whenever HTMT is high.
